What is Big Walk?

Big Walk is a co-op puzzle-adventure game by House House, the studio behind Untitled Goose Game, published by Panic. It launched on August 4, 2026 for PC, macOS, PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2, with crossplay across all platforms.

The game is online multiplayer only — there is no single-player mode and no split-screen. Each session supports between 2 and 12 players. There are no quest markers, no health bars and almost no instructions: you and your friends must explore, communicate and solve puzzles together.

  • 2–12 players per session, cross-platform
  • Proximity voice chat that fades with distance
  • Four colored towers to unlock, then a final Black Tower
  • Two endings to discover

Quick Start Tips

Set your lobby size correctly

Some puzzles require four players. A duo or trio with the wrong lobby size may spawn puzzles they cannot complete, so check your group size before starting.

Find a light source early

The day/night cycle is very fast and nights are extremely dark. Grab a flashlight or lantern as soon as you can.

Use backpacks and belts

Found in blue structures, each adds one extra item slot. Items stored there will not drop when you fall.

Focus on one tower at a time

After the tutorial, pick a single tower and finish it before moving to the next. Most guides recommend starting with the Red Tower.
