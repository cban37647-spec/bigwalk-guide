Big Walk All Endings

How to unlock both endings in Big Walk, and what you need to complete first.

How Many Endings Are There?

Big Walk has two endings. The second ending is locked behind the first, so you must finish the game once before you can access the alternative ending.

Ending 1 — Completing the Black Tower

The first ending is reached by completing all four colored towers and then finishing the final Black Tower. The Black Tower requires six rewards (one more than the other towers), and voice chat is disabled in three of its six trials.

After the Black Tower, you receive the Black Key, which opens the Green Wall gates and leads to the game's conclusion.

Ending 2 — The Locked Alternative

The second ending is locked behind the first. Once you have completed the main ending, the path to the alternative becomes available on a subsequent run. The exact trigger is still being mapped by the community.

Check back as more players reach the second ending and document the steps.
