Big Walk Red Tower Puzzle Solutions

All five Red Tower puzzles solved, plus how to open the Map Room and get the folded map.

Red Tower Overview

The Red Tower is the first tower most players tackle. You need to complete five puzzles to gather five MacGuffins, then climb the tower, take the key and open the Map Room to receive the folded map reward.

All Five Red Tower Puzzles

1. Yellow Maze Puzzle

One player takes the left path and grabs a wind-up timer, then passes it through a hole to the other player on the right path. The second player places it on the green pot before time runs out.

2. Green Button Room

One player is inside the room and calls out which button lights up. The player outside presses the matching button to complete the sequence.

3. Train Station Timer Cannon

One player fires the timer from the cannon. The other player chases and catches it, then throws it back so it can be placed in the green pot before the timer expires.

4. Basketball Court

Throw the light-up bulbs or balls through the baskets until all of the lights turn green.

5. Mountain Overlook Gesture

One player stays at the overlook with the telescopes. The other visits five platforms below and performs the shown gesture. The observer selects the matching gesture card for each one.

Opening the Map Room

After finishing all five puzzles, climb the Red Tower, take the key and use the key cutters. Then open the Map Room to collect the folded map.

The map reward is the main reason to do the Red Tower first — it makes exploring the rest of the island far easier.
