Big Walk MacGuffin Locations

What the "eggy things" are, how many you need, and how to gather them for each tower.

What Are MacGuffins?

MacGuffins are the collectible items you need to complete each colored tower. Players often call them "eggy things" — they look like little red gourd-shaped collectibles. You gather them by solving communication and co-op puzzles, then place enough of them into a tower's console to unlock a key and a major reward.

Where to Find Them

MacGuffins are not hidden in random corners — each one is the reward for solving a specific puzzle tied to its tower. For example, the Red Tower's five MacGuffins come from its five puzzles: the Yellow Maze, the Green Button Room, the Train Station Timer Cannon, the Basketball Court and the Mountain Overlook Gesture.

The best approach is to work through one tower at a time. Each puzzle you solve drops a MacGuffin you can carry back to that tower's console. Use your backpack and belt to hold extra items so they do not drop when you fall.
