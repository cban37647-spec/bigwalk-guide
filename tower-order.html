Big Walk Tower Order

Which of the four colored towers should you do first, and what each one unlocks.

The Best Tower Order

Big Walk has four colored towers — Red, Blue, Green and Yellow — plus a final Black Tower that only opens after all four are complete. There is no fixed order, but most guides recommend a clear sequence.

Order Tower Reward Why
1 Red Tower Map Room (folded map) You spawn near it, and the map makes everything else much easier.
2 Blue Tower Train network The train helps you cover long distances, though it is slow and one-way.
3 Green Tower Chairlift Another travel option that connects parts of the island.
4 Yellow Tower Tunnels Tunnels give much faster access across the island.
5 Black Tower Black Key Only unlocks after all four colored towers are complete.

Why Start With the Red Tower?

The Red Tower is the natural first choice for a simple reason: you spawn near it, and its reward — the Map Room — gives you a folded map of the island. Once you have the map, exploring and planning the remaining towers becomes dramatically easier.

Each colored tower requires five "eggy things" (MacGuffins) to complete. Gather them by solving co-op communication puzzles, then place them into the tower's console.

About the Black Tower

The Black Tower is a dark monolith near the chairlift. It only opens after you finish all four colored towers, and it requires six parts instead of five. Voice chat is disabled in three of its six trials, so plan your communication signals in advance.
